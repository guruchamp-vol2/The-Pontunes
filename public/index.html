ABOUT

From Washington.
Across the Northwest.

The Pontunes, also spelled The PonTunes, formed in Issaquah and Bellingham, Washington, and have been active across the Pacific Northwest for decades.

Their music brings together rock and roll, vocal harmony pop, Americana, R&B, jazz, and original material.

THE MUSIC

A wide-ranging
repertoire.

  • Rock & roll
  • Vocal harmony pop
  • Americana
  • R&B
  • Jazz
  • Originals

Their repertoire includes songs by The Beatles, Neil Young, J.J. Cale, Van Morrison, Frank Sinatra, Eric Clapton, and NRBQ, alongside jazz standards and original music.
